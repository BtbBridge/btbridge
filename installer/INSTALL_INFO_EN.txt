BTBridge — Windows PC -> Android Low-Latency Audio Bridge

This wizard installs the BTBridge desktop application, Android connection tools (ADB), and the Android companion app (BTBridge.apk). Python, JDK, or Android Studio are NOT required.

QUICK START (USB CABLE — LOWEST LATENCY):
1. On your Android phone, enable "USB Debugging" under Settings > Developer Options.
2. Connect your phone via USB cable and tap "Allow" on the USB debugging prompt.
3. Launch BTBridge from the desktop shortcut and click "Install APK to Phone" (or check the install option at the end of setup).
4. Click "Start Streaming" on your PC (your PC speakers/headphones are routed to a silent channel automatically).
5. Open BTBridge on your phone and tap "Connect via USB".

LOCAL WI-FI MODE (WIRELESS):
1. Make sure your PC and phone are on the same Wi-Fi network.
2. Select "Local Wi-Fi" on the PC app and click "Start Streaming".
3. Enter the displayed local IP address in the Android app and tap "Connect via Wi-Fi".

SYSTEM TRAY TIP:
- Clicking the "X" button minimizes BTBridge to the Windows system tray and keeps streaming audio in the background.
- To exit completely, right-click the BTBridge tray icon and select "Quit Completely".
